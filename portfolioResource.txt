Using GitHub to create a portfolio for job searches could be a useful benefit
both to myself and future employers.  By adding completed projects to GitHub, I
could measure my own growth and make wise choices about which roles I qualify
for before applying.  Continually working on new projects and growing the 
online portfolio is another way to stay current and increase knowledge.

Having a well organized portfolio demonstrates to potential employers an ability
to self manage, complete projects, and produce quality work.  It also shows
how long it took to finish a program and how problem solving was achieved.  

Companies have existing software and may require someone to handle maintenance 
or work on a program started by someone else.  Collaborating on GitHub is a 
real world way to demonstrate the ability to work on a team.
