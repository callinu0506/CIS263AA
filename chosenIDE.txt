After researching and comparing features of different IDEs, I decided to use NetBeans.  Originally, I was 
leaning toward IntelliJ but the school was not on the approved list for the free version and it would take
several days for approval so I decided to pick either Eclipse or NetBeans.  A side by side comparison showed
NetBeans had more advantages for me as a user, while Eclipse seemed more complicated and slow.  I wanted 
less complexity, beginner friendly where I could focus more on learning Java, than trouble shooting new
software.  Mostly, Java I labs were completed within Zybooks so it already feels a little bit like starting
over by moving to strictly IDE.  NetBeans features seem the best fit for my comfort level.
